Before you borrow

  • You can hold up to 3 books at a time.
  • Loans last 7 working days (Sundays and holidays don't count).
  • Overdue books cost ₱2 per working day, per book.
  • Requests are denied if you have overdue books, unpaid fines, or a suspension.
  • Show your school ID at the desk when you pick up a physical book.
