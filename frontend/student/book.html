Borrowing rules

  • Hold up to 3 books at a time.
  • Each book has 1 copy, so only one student can have it.
  • Loans run 7 working days. Saturdays count; Sundays and holidays don't.
  • Renew up to 3 times. A renewal resets the loan to 7 days left.
  • Late books cost ₱2 per working day, per book.
  • Show your school ID when you pick up a physical book.
